Free calculators for daily budgeting and maths

Household maths is mostly small sums done often: a 15% discount on a 2,000 bill saves 300; splitting a 4,500 dinner bill four ways is 1,125 each; a monthly budget is just income minus fixed costs, tracked weekly. A free calculators for daily budgeting and maths portal keeps the everyday ones — percentage, loan, age, BMI, tip-split and unit conversion — in one place, with step-by-step tutorials so you can check the working, not just the answer.

Punjab vehicle checks before you buy

Never pay for a used vehicle before checking its record: enter the registration number on the MTMIS Punjab portal, match the chassis and engine numbers against the smart card, and confirm token-tax status and any pending e-challan. This Punjab smart card status check guide walks through vehicle verification, registration, token tax and transfer step by step, so a five-minute check can save you from a stolen or token-defaulter vehicle.
